About Our Company

Building value across multiple industries.

SJAKKO Ogundare Global Resources Nig Ltd is a Nigerian registered company established to operate across a diverse range of commercial and industrial sectors.

Our business interests include oil and gas, transportation and logistics, construction, agriculture and food processing, information technology, automobiles and equipment, general trading, import and export, hospitality and other professional services.
